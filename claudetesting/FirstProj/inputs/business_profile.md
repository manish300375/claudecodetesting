# Business Profile (dummy company for testing)

- **Company name / website:** BrightBean Coffee (fictional, brightbean.example)
- **What we sell:** Freshly roasted specialty coffee beans by subscription (weekly, biweekly, monthly), plus brewing gear add-ons.
- **Target customers:** Home coffee drinkers aged 25-45 in the US who want better-than-supermarket coffee with convenience. Mostly urban, mid-to-high income.
- **Geography / markets:** United States, ships nationwide.
- **Pricing model & price points:** Subscription $16-$22 per 12 oz bag; free shipping over $30; 10% off for subscribers.
- **Key differentiators:** Small-batch roasting shipped within 48 hours of roasting; flexible skip/pause; direct-trade sourcing story.
- **Known weaknesses:** Small brand awareness; limited roast variety (6 coffees); no mobile app; few reviews.
- **Known competitors:** Trade Coffee (to verify)
- **Goals for this analysis:** Understand how top coffee-subscription competitors position, price and retain customers; find 3-5 concrete improvements for the next quarter.
