# BrightBean Coffee: Brand Guidelines (dummy, for testing)

**Voice:** warm, knowledgeable, unpretentious.

## Logo
Coffee-bean mark in Espresso Brown with an Amber highlight, plus the "BrightBean" wordmark in Georgia Bold. Keep clear space equal to the bean's height. Use `logo.png` on white and `logo_light.png` (white wordmark) on Espresso backgrounds.

## Colors
| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Espresso | #3E2723 |
| Secondary | Mocha | #8D6E63 |
| Accent | Amber | #E8A33D |
| Text | Charcoal | #2B2B2B |
| Background | White | #FFFFFF |
| Muted panel | Cream | #F6EFE7 |

## Typography
- Headings: Georgia Bold
- Body: Verdana, 10pt, generous line spacing
